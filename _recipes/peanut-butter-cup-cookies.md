---
title: Peanut Butter Cup Cookies
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Peanut Butter
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Cookies
  - Peanut Butter
  - Chocolate
description: Peanut butter cookie cups with a miniature Reese's peanut butter cup pressed into the center.
cook_time: 8 min
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 30-40 cookies
ingredients:
  - 1-3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  - 1/2 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1/2 cup butter, softened
  - 1/2 cup white sugar
  - 1/2 cup peanut butter
  - 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  - 1 egg, beaten
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 2 tablespoons milk
  - About 30-40 miniature Reese's peanut butter cups, unwrapped
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 375°F. Whisk together flour, salt, and baking soda; set aside.
  - Cream together butter, white sugar, peanut butter, and brown sugar until fluffy. Beat in egg, vanilla, and milk. Add flour mixture and mix well.
  - Shape into 1 inch balls and place each into a greased mini muffin tin.
  - Bake at 375°F for about 8 minutes. Do not overbake!
  - Remove from oven and immediately press a mini peanut butter cup into each ball. Cool and carefully remove from pan.

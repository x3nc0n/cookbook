---
title: Pomme Purée
dish_type: Side
cuisine: French
main_ingredient: Potato
tags:
  - Side
  - French
  - Potato
  - Butter
description: Extra buttery mashed potatoes inspired by Joël Robuchon's iconic recipe — richer, creamier, and smoother than traditional mashed potatoes.
cook_time: 45 min
prep_time: 30 min
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - 3 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, rinsed and scrubbed
  - Salt
  - 1-3/4 cups whole milk
  - 2 cloves garlic, halved
  - 1 pound butter, cut into 1-inch chunks
  - 1/2 teaspoon finely ground white pepper
instructions:
  - Place potatoes in a single layer in a large pot. Cover with at least 1 inch of water. Season with 1 tablespoon salt. Simmer over medium heat for 40-45 minutes or until easily pierced with a knife. Transfer to an ice bath to cool slightly. Discard water and wipe pot clean.
  - Once cool enough to handle, peel the potatoes. Put through a food mill or potato ricer into a mixing bowl. Set aside.
  - Place milk in the pot and bring to a simmer. Add garlic and steep a few minutes until fragrant. Discard garlic, reduce heat to low. Whisk in butter a few chunks at a time until well incorporated.
  - Add riced potatoes; stir with a wooden spoon or spatula until smooth. Season with 2 teaspoons salt and white pepper.
  - Serve hot. To hold for later, pour into a casserole dish, cover with foil, and keep warm in a 250°F oven.

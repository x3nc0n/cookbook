---
title: Margarita Cheesecake
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Cream Cheese
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Frozen
  - Vegetarian
description: A frozen no-bake margarita cheesecake with tequila and lime.
prep_time: 15m
total_time: 8h 15m
servings: '8'
ingredients:
  - 6 oz frozen margarita mix
  - 14 oz sweetened condensed milk
  - 8 oz cream cheese
  - 1 shot tequila
  - Lime zest
  - 8 oz whipped cream, thawed
  - Pie crust
  - Limes
  - Sparkling sugar
instructions:
  - In a large bowl, combine margarita mix, condensed milk, and cream cheese, mixing until creamy and smooth.
  - Mix in tequila, lime zest, and whipped cream.
  - Pour mixture into pie crust.
  - Freeze for 8 hours, or overnight.
  - Garnish with limes and sparkling sugar.
  - Serve and enjoy!

---
title: Nutty Cranberry Relish
dish_type: Side
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Cranberries
tags:
  - Side
  - American
  - Thanksgiving
  - Holiday
  - Cranberry
description: A sweet-tart cranberry relish with fresh orange, apple, and toasted pecans.
cook_time: 22 min
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - 1 (12 oz) bag fresh or frozen cranberries
  - 1 cup sugar, plus more to taste
  - 1 strip orange or lemon zest
  - 2 tablespoons water
  - 1 unpeeled orange, quartered and seeded
  - 1 tart apple, unpeeled, quartered and seeded
  - 1/2 cup sugar (for the fruit)
  - 1/2 cup chopped toasted pecans
  - Salt and pepper to taste
instructions:
  - Reserve 1/2 cup cranberries in a small bowl. Put the rest in a saucepan with 1 cup sugar, citrus zest, and water. Cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until sugar dissolves and cranberries are soft, about 10 minutes.
  - Increase heat to medium and cook until cranberries burst, about 12 minutes. Reduce heat to low and stir in reserved cranberries. Season with sugar, salt, and pepper to taste.
  - Quarter the orange and apple (unpeeled, seeds removed). Roughly chop in a food processor with 1/2 cup sugar.
  - Fold fruit mixture into cranberry sauce along with toasted pecans. Let cool to room temperature before serving.

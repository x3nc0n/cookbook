---
title: Chicken Piccata
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: Italian
main_ingredient: Chicken
tags:
  - Entrée
  - Italian
  - Chicken
  - Quick
description: Pan-fried chicken cutlets in a lemon-butter caper sauce with white wine.
cook_time: 20 min
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - 2 skinless boneless chicken breasts (about 1-1/2 pounds total)
  - 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  - 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  - 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  - 1/3 cup dry white wine
  - 3/4 cup chicken stock
  - 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  - 1 tablespoon drained capers
  - Handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
instructions:
  - Cut each chicken breast in half horizontally. Pound each piece between parchment paper to 1/4 to 1/8 inch thickness.
  - Combine flour with salt and pepper on a large plate. Dredge each piece of chicken through seasoned flour.
  - Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté chicken in batches until golden brown, about 3 minutes per side. Set aside on a warm plate.
  - Reduce heat to low. Add 3 tablespoons butter and garlic; sauté until golden, 1-2 minutes. Add wine and reduce by half. Add stock and cook to reduce, 4 minutes.
  - Stir in lemon juice, capers, remaining butter, and parsley. Shimmy the skillet to help sauce emulsify. Let thicken slightly, about 2 minutes.
  - Return chicken to pan and toss gently to coat. Serve with sauce poured overtop.

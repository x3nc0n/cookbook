---
title: Belgian Hot Chocolate
dish_type: Beverage
cuisine: Belgian
main_ingredient: Chocolate
tags:
  - Beverage
  - Belgian
  - Chocolate
  - Winter
description: Rich Belgian-style hot chocolate made with bittersweet and milk chocolate, served with optional whipped cream and chocolate curls.
cook_time: 10 min
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - 1 quart half-and-half or whole milk
  - 8 oz bittersweet or semisweet chocolate, chopped
  - 4 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  - Tiny pinch of salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
instructions:
  - In a medium saucepan, warm about one-third of the half-and-half with the chopped chocolates and salt, stirring until the chocolate is melted.
  - Whisk in the remaining half-and-half, heating until warmed through. Add the cinnamon.
  - Use a hand-held blender or whisk to mix until completely smooth. Serve very warm.
  - "Optional: top with whipped cream and chocolate curls. Can be stored in the refrigerator for up to one week."

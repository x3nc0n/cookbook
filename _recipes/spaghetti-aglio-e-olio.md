---
title: Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
dish_type: Entrée
tags:
- Entrée
- Italian
- Pasta
- Vegetarian
cuisine: Italian
main_ingredient: Pasta
description: A simple and classic Italian pasta dish with garlic, olive oil, and chili flakes.
ingredients:
- 1 lb spaghetti
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt to taste
- Freshly grated parmesan cheese
instructions:
- Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before
  draining.
- While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic slices and cook until golden, about 2
  minutes. Be careful not to burn.
- Add red pepper flakes and cook 30 seconds more.
- Add drained pasta to the skillet along with 1/2 cup reserved pasta water. Toss to coat.
- Add parsley, toss again, and season with salt.
- Serve immediately topped with freshly grated parmesan.

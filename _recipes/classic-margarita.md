description: A perfectly balanced classic margarita with fresh lime juice.
prep_time: 5m
servings: '1'
ingredients:
  - 2 oz tequila blanco
  - 1 oz fresh lime juice
  - 1 oz triple sec (Cointreau)
  - Salt for rimming
  - Lime wheel for garnish
  - Ice
instructions:
  - Rim a rocks glass with salt by running a lime wedge around the edge and dipping in salt.
  - Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
  - Add tequila, lime juice, and triple sec to the shaker.
  - Shake vigorously for 15 seconds until well chilled.
  - Strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice.
  - Garnish with a lime wheel and serve immediately.

---
title: Triple Berry Baked Oatmeal
dish_type: Breakfast
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Oats
tags:
  - Breakfast
  - American
  - Healthy
  - Berries
description: A light and healthy baked oatmeal with blueberries, blackberries, and strawberries. Lower in sugar than traditional baked oatmeal.
cook_time: 20 min
prep_time: 5 min
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - 2 cups old fashioned oats
  - 1-1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon salt
  - 1-1/2 cups milk (unsweetened vanilla almond milk works great)
  - 1/4 cup maple syrup
  - 1 large egg
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 2 cups mix of blueberries, blackberries, and cut up strawberries
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a medium baking dish.
  - In a medium bowl, mix together oats, baking powder, and salt. Add milk, maple syrup, egg, and vanilla. Stir well. Stir in berries.
  - Pour mixture into casserole dish. Bake for 20 minutes. Enjoy!

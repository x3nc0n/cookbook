description: Filet mignon topped with asparagus, lump crab, and a tarragon-lemon béarnaise-style sauce.
prep_time: 45m
cook_time: 55m
total_time: 1h 40m
servings: '2'
ingredients:
  - 2 center-cut filet mignon steaks (6 to 8 ounces each), trimmed
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  - 8 ounces asparagus, woody ends trimmed
  - 1/2 pound jumbo lump crabmeat
  - 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  - 1/2 large shallot, thinly sliced
  - 2 sprigs fresh tarragon, leaves minced and stems reserved
  - 1 tablespoon minced fresh chives, plus more for garnish
  - 1/4 teaspoon grated lemon zest
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
  - 1/4 cup dry white wine
  - 1/4 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  - 1 large egg yolk
  - 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  - Pinch of red pepper flakes
  - Pinch of cayenne pepper
  - Pinch of paprika (optional)
instructions:
  - Pat the steaks dry, season all over with salt and pepper, and let them sit at room temperature while you prep the remaining components.
  - Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Blanch the asparagus until crisp-tender, then transfer to ice water, drain, and set aside.
  - In a small saucepan, combine the shallot, tarragon stems, vinegar, white wine, and peppercorns. Simmer until the liquid is reduced to a few tablespoons, then strain.
  - Melt 4 tablespoons of the butter and keep it warm. Whisk the egg yolk with the strained reduction over very gentle heat, then slowly whisk in the melted butter until the sauce is thick and glossy. Stir in the minced tarragon, chives, lemon zest, and lemon juice; season with salt and black pepper.
  - Warm 1 tablespoon of the remaining butter in a skillet over low heat. Add the garlic, red pepper flakes, cayenne, paprika if using, and crabmeat, then heat gently just until the crab is warmed through.
  - Heat the oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the steaks until well browned on both sides, then finish to your preferred doneness and rest for several minutes.
  - Melt the last tablespoon of butter and toss it with the asparagus to rewarm and lightly coat.
  - To serve, place asparagus on each steak, spoon the warm crab over the top, and finish with the tarragon sauce and extra chives.

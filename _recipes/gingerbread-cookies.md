---
title: Gingerbread Cookies
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Flour
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Cookies
  - Holiday
  - Family Recipe
description: Grandma Zakrzewicz's soft gingerbread cookies with molasses and sour cream. A soft, bread-type cookie.
cook_time: 9-10 min
prep_time: 4h+ (chilling)
servings: several dozen
ingredients:
  - 1 cup shortening
  - 2 cups sugar
  - 2 eggs
  - 1 cup molasses
  - 1 cup sour cream
  - 6 cups flour
  - 2 teaspoons baking soda
  - 1 teaspoon cloves
  - 1 teaspoon nutmeg
  - 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  - 1 teaspoon salt
instructions:
  - In a large bowl, cream together shortening and sugar. Add eggs and mix well.
  - Add molasses and sour cream; mix to combine.
  - In another large bowl, combine flour, baking soda, cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, and salt.
  - Gradually add dry ingredients to the sugar mixture.
  - Refrigerate at least 4 hours, or overnight.
  - Roll out on floured surface, working with about 1/3 of the dough at a time (keep the rest refrigerated). Cut into shapes.
  - Bake at 350°F for 9-10 minutes. Use parchment paper to preserve shapes. Don't overbake — this is a soft, bread-type cookie.

---
title: Chicken Tortilla Soup
dish_type: Soup
cuisine: Mexican
main_ingredient: Chicken
tags:
  - Soup
  - Mexican
  - Chicken
  - Slow Cooker
  - Gluten-Free
description: A slow cooker chicken tortilla soup loaded with tomatoes, chilies, and spices, topped with cheese, avocado, and crushed tortilla chips.
cook_time: 5-6h
prep_time: 15 min
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 1 (46 oz) can chicken broth
  - 1 (15 oz) can tomato sauce
  - 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
  - 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie works great)
  - 2 anaheim chilies, diced
  - 1 jalapeño pepper, diced
  - 1/2 cup diced onion
  - 3-4 large tomatoes, diced
  - 2 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 tablespoons minced cilantro
  - 1 tablespoon chili powder
  - 2 teaspoons cumin
  - 2 teaspoons pepper
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 2 teaspoons Tabasco sauce (chipotle flavor recommended)
  - 1/2 teaspoon chipotle pepper (optional)
  - "Garnish: 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese"
  - "Garnish: 1 avocado, chopped"
  - "Garnish: 1/4 cup cilantro"
  - "Garnish: 1 lime, cut into wedges"
  - "Garnish: 2 cups crushed tortilla chips"
instructions:
  - Throw everything except the garnishes in your crock pot and let it cook on high for 5-6 hours.
  - Dress with garnishes and give it a squeeze of lime and you're good to go!
  - "If not using a crock pot: sauté the garlic and onion in 2 tablespoons olive oil before adding the remaining ingredients. Simmer for at least an hour."

---
title: Dairy-Free Cream of Chicken Soup
dish_type: Soup
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chicken
tags:
  - Soup
  - American
  - Chicken
  - Dairy-Free
  - Gluten-Free Option
description: A homemade dairy-free cream of chicken soup using almond milk and chicken broth as a base.
cook_time: 15 min
prep_time: 5 min
servings: 4 cups
ingredients:
  - 2-1/2 cups organic chicken broth
  - 1-1/2 cups almond milk
  - 3/4 cup flour (gluten-free if needed)
  - 1 teaspoon real salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  - 1/2 teaspoon parsley
  - 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  - 1/4 teaspoon basil
  - 1/4 cup cooked mushrooms (optional)
  - 1/4 cup cooked celery (optional)
instructions:
  - Combine the chicken broth with 1/2 cup of the almond milk in a large saucepan. Bring to a low boil.
  - Whisk the flour and seasonings into the remaining cup of almond milk until smooth. The mixture will be thick.
  - Pour the milk/flour/seasoning mixture into the broth mixture and reduce heat to low. Stir/whisk continuously as it simmers to avoid lumps.
  - Continue whisking 5-10 minutes until the mixture is smooth and thick.

---
title: Traditional Irish Stew
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: Irish
main_ingredient: Beef
tags:

description: A hearty traditional Irish stew with seared beef, mushrooms, carrots, celery, and red potatoes in a rich roux-thickened broth.
cook_time: 1h
prep_time: 20 min
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 1 pound beef (chuck shoulder or stew meat), cut into 1 inch chunks
  - Salt and pepper
  - 1-2 tablespoons avocado oil
  - 1 white or yellow onion, cut into large chunks
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 3 tablespoons butter
  - 3 tablespoons white rice flour (or all-purpose flour)
  - 3 cups low sodium beef broth
  - A few sprigs of thyme (or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme)
  - 16 oz mushrooms, washed and quartered
  - 4 stalks celery, cut into large chunks
  - 5 carrots, peeled and cut into large chunks
  - 10 red potatoes (2 pounds), cut into big chunks
  - 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  - Black pepper to taste
  - Fresh parsley for garnish
instructions:
  - Pat beef dry, season with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Sear beef in batches (don't overcrowd), 2-3 minutes per side. Remove to a dish.
  - Add onion and a little salt; sauté 3 minutes until tender. Add garlic and sauté 1 minute more.
  - Melt butter, add flour, and cook the roux 1-2 minutes until lightly browned.
  - Slowly whisk in about 1/2 cup broth, scraping the bottom of the pan. Stir in remaining broth, beef, and any juices. Add thyme sprigs. Cover and simmer 20 minutes.
  - Add mushrooms and simmer 15 minutes until reduced in size.
  - Add carrots and celery; simmer 10 minutes uncovered.
  - Stir in salt and pepper, add potatoes, cover and simmer 10-12 minutes until potatoes are fork tender.
  - Remove thyme sprigs. Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with parsley. Serve immediately. Leftovers keep up to 1 week refrigerated.

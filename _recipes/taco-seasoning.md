---
title: Taco Seasoning
dish_type: Seasoning
cuisine: Mexican
main_ingredient: Spices
tags:
  - Seasoning
  - Mexican
  - Spices
  - Pantry Staple
description: A homemade taco seasoning blend to replace store-bought packets.
prep_time: 5 min
servings: about 1/3 cup
ingredients:
  - 4 tablespoons chili powder
  - 2 tablespoons cumin
  - 2 teaspoons onion powder
  - 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  - 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  - 2 teaspoons oregano
  - 2 teaspoons salt
  - 4 teaspoons black pepper
  - Pinch cayenne pepper (optional)
instructions:
  - Combine all ingredients and mix well. Store in an airtight container.
  - Use about 2 tablespoons per pound of meat.

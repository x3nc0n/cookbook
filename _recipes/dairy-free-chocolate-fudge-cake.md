---
title: Dairy-Free Chocolate Fudge Cake
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chocolate
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Chocolate
  - Dairy-Free
  - Vegan
description: A fudgy three-layer dairy-free chocolate cake with a rich coconut milk chocolate frosting.
cook_time: 35-45 min
prep_time: 30 min + overnight (frosting)
servings: 10
ingredients:
  - "Frosting: 30 oz full-fat coconut milk (2 cans)"
  - "Frosting: 3 cups dairy-free chocolate chunks"
  - "Frosting: 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted"
  - "Frosting: 2 cups powdered sugar"
  - "Cake: 3 cups whole wheat flour"
  - "Cake: 1-1/2 cups dark cocoa powder"
  - "Cake: 1 cup sugar"
  - "Cake: 1 tablespoon baking soda"
  - "Cake: 1-1/2 teaspoons baking powder"
  - "Cake: 1 pinch salt"
  - "Cake: 3 cups almond milk"
  - "Cake: 1 cup coconut oil, melted"
  - "Cake: 1-1/2 cups applesauce"
  - "Cake: 1-1/2 cups maple syrup"
  - "Cake: 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar"
  - "Cake: 1 tablespoon vanilla extract"
  - Berries for garnish
instructions:
  - "Frosting (make ahead): Heat coconut milk until hot but not boiling. Pour over dairy-free chocolate in a large bowl; stir to melt. Add melted coconut oil and powdered sugar; beat until smooth. Cover and refrigerate overnight."
  - Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease 3 separate 8-inch round cake pans.
  - Whisk together whole wheat flour, dark cocoa powder, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  - In a separate bowl, whisk together almond milk, coconut oil, applesauce, maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, and vanilla.
  - Fold dry mixture into wet in two batches until combined (some clumps are okay).
  - Divide batter between the 3 pans. Bake 35-45 minutes or until a toothpick comes out almost clean. Cool completely.
  - Remove frosting from fridge and mix well. Frost between layers, then frost the top and sides generously. Top with berries.

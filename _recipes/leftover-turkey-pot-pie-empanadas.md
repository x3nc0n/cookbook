---
title: Leftover Turkey Pot Pie Empanadas
dish_type: Appetizer
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Turkey
tags:
  - Appetizer
  - American
  - Turkey
  - Thanksgiving
  - Leftovers
description: Delicious hand pies using leftover Thanksgiving turkey with peas, carrots, and a savory broth filling in empanada discs.
cook_time: 30 min
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 10 empanadas
ingredients:
  - 1 teaspoon unsalted butter
  - 1/4 cup chopped onion
  - 1/4 cup chopped celery
  - 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 1 tablespoon corn starch
  - 1/2 cup low sodium chicken or turkey broth
  - 1-1/4 cups leftover shredded cooked turkey breast (5 oz)
  - 3/4 cup frozen peas and carrots
  - 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  - 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper
  - 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  - 10 frozen Goya Empanada Discos (for baking), thawed
  - Cooking spray
  - 1 large egg, whisked
instructions:
  - Heat a skillet over medium heat, melt butter. Add onion, celery, and parsley; cook until onions are translucent. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  - Combine broth and cornstarch; mix well.
  - Add turkey, frozen peas and carrots, salt, pepper, thyme, and broth mixture. Bring to a boil then cook until thickened, 2-3 minutes.
  - Place a disc on work surface. Place 2 tablespoons turkey mixture in the center. Brush outer edges with egg wash. Fold disc over and press edges to seal; use a fork to crimp.
  - Brush egg wash over one side of each empanada.
  - Spray baking sheet with oil. Bake at 350°F for 26-30 minutes until golden.
  - "Air Fryer option: Spray basket with oil, cook at 380°F for 8 minutes, turning halfway."

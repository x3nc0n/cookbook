---
title: One Pot Taco Skillet
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: Mexican
main_ingredient: Beef
tags:
  - Entrée
  - Mexican
  - Beef
  - Quick
  - One-Pot
description: A quick one-pot taco skillet with ground beef, poblano peppers, rice, and fire-roasted tomatoes.
cook_time: 25 min
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - 1 tablespoon oil
  - 1 poblano, deseeded and diced
  - 1/2 yellow onion, diced
  - 3/4 lb ground beef
  - 2 tablespoons taco seasoning
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1/2 cup white rice
  - 1 (14 oz) can fire roasted tomatoes
  - 1/4 cup water
  - "Toppings: shredded cheese, avocado, cilantro, lime juice, tortilla chips"
instructions:
  - Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add onion and poblano peppers; sauté 5-6 minutes until soft and beginning to brown.
  - Add ground beef, taco seasoning, and minced garlic. Cook until meat begins to brown, about 3 minutes.
  - Add rice, stirring to mix into the beef and veggie mixture. Add tomatoes with juices and 1/4 cup water. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes. Avoid opening the lid until the end.
  - Remove from heat. Serve topped with cheese, avocado, cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and tortilla chips on the side.

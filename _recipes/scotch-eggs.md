---
title: Scotch Eggs
dish_type: Appetizer
cuisine: British
main_ingredient: Eggs
tags:
  - Appetizer
  - British
  - Eggs
  - Sausage
  - Deep-Fried
description: Classic British Scotch eggs — hard-boiled eggs wrapped in seasoned sausage, breaded, and deep-fried until golden.
cook_time: 30 min
prep_time: 30 min + 30 min chill
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 6 whole eggs in shells, plus 1 lightly beaten
  - 1 lb ground pork sausage
  - 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  - 1 tablespoon English mustard
  - 2 teaspoons cornstarch
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground mace
  - 2 leaves sage, finely chopped
  - 2 sprigs thyme, finely chopped
  - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - Canola oil, for frying
  - 1/4 cup milk
  - 1/2 cup flour
  - 2 cups panko bread crumbs
instructions:
  - Place 6 eggs in a saucepan, cover by 1 inch with cold water. Bring to a boil over high heat; cover, remove from heat, let sit 6 minutes. Drain, transfer to ice water for 5 minutes. Peel and set aside.
  - Combine sausage, Worcestershire, mustard, cornstarch, mace, sage, and thyme; season with salt and pepper. Divide into 6 portions and form each around an egg to cover completely. Refrigerate 30 minutes.
  - Pour oil to a depth of 2 inches in a Dutch oven and heat to 350°F. Combine beaten egg with milk in a bowl. Place flour and bread crumbs in separate bowls.
  - Coat each sausage-covered egg in flour, dip in egg-milk mixture, then dredge in bread crumbs. Fry in batches until golden brown and cooked through, about 7 minutes. Drain on paper towels and cool 10 minutes before serving.

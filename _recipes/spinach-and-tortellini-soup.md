---
title: Spinach and Tortellini Soup
dish_type: Soup
cuisine: Italian
main_ingredient: Tortellini
tags:
  - Soup
  - Italian
  - Vegetarian
  - Tortellini
  - Quick
description: A quick 20-minute Italian soup with cheese tortellini and fresh spinach in a tomato-vegetable broth.
cook_time: 20 min
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 1 teaspoon olive oil
  - 2 garlic cloves, minced
  - 1 can (14-1/2 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes, undrained
  - 3 cans (14-1/2 oz each) vegetable broth
  - 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  - 1 package (9 oz) refrigerated cheese tortellini
  - 4 cups fresh baby spinach
  - Shredded Parmesan cheese
  - Freshly ground pepper
instructions:
  - In a large saucepan, heat oil over medium heat. Add garlic; cook and stir 1 minute. Stir in tomatoes, broth and Italian seasoning; bring to a boil.
  - Add tortellini; bring to a gentle boil. Cook uncovered until tortellini are tender, 7-9 minutes.
  - Stir in spinach. Sprinkle servings with Parmesan cheese and pepper.

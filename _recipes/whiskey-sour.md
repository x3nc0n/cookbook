---
title: Whiskey Sour
dish_type: Beverage
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Bourbon
tags:
  - Beverage
  - American
  - Cocktail
  - Bourbon
description: The cocktail bar standard whiskey sour with an egg white for a silky foam, plus a classic no-egg variation.
cook_time: 5 min
servings: 1
ingredients:
  - 2 oz bourbon
  - 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
  - 0.75 oz simple syrup
  - 1 egg white
  - Angostura Bitters
  - Lemon peel
instructions:
  - Add all ingredients to a shaker tin. Dry shake (without ice) for five seconds to whip the egg white.
  - Add ice, seal tins, and shake hard for 10-12 seconds.
  - Strain into a coupe or martini glass. The color will emerge over a minute under a paper-smooth head of foam.
  - Express a lemon peel over the foam for aroma and discard. Decorate foam with a few drops of Angostura Bitters.
  - "Classic variation (no egg): Use 2 oz Irish or Canadian whiskey, 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice, 0.75 oz simple syrup. Shake with ice 6-10 seconds. Strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass. Garnish with orange slice and/or cherry."

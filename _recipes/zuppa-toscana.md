---
title: Zuppa Toscana
dish_type: Soup
cuisine: Italian
main_ingredient: Sausage
tags:
  - Soup
  - Italian
  - Sausage
  - Potato
  - Comfort Food
description: A creamy Italian soup with sausage, potatoes, kale, and bacon in a rich broth.
cook_time: 1h
servings: 4-6
ingredients:
  - 1 lb Italian sausage (mild recommended)
  - 2 large russet baking potatoes, washed, halved, and sliced 1/4 inch thick
  - 1 large Vidalia onion, chopped
  - 4 slices cooked bacon, chopped
  - 1 teaspoon Accent seasoning (optional)
  - 1/2 teaspoon salt
  - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  - 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  - 2 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 cups kale or Swiss chard, chopped
  - 2 (8 oz) cans chicken broth
  - 1 quart water
  - 2 teaspoons flour or cornstarch, mixed with water to make a paste
  - 1 cup heavy whipping cream (tempered)
instructions:
  - Chop or slice uncooked sausage into small pieces and cook until browned and ground in the bottom of your soup pan over medium-high heat.
  - Add onions, garlic, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes to sausage and cook until onions are clear and aromatics blend together.
  - Reduce heat to medium and add potatoes. Slowly add chicken stock and water; stir.
  - Cook on medium heat until potatoes are done, stirring occasionally. Once potatoes are done, add flour or cornstarch paste and simmer for another 15 minutes.
  - Reduce heat to lowest setting and sprinkle in kale and cooked bacon. Slowly pour cream while stirring.
  - Check for taste and then serve.

---
title: Baked Eggnog French Toast
dish_type: Breakfast
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Bread
tags:
  - Breakfast
  - American
  - Holiday
  - Make-Ahead
description: Golden egg-rich challah soaked in a bourbon-spiked eggnog custard, assembled the night before and baked until puffy and browned.
cook_time: 50 min
prep_time: 15 min
servings: 6-8
ingredients:
  - 1 loaf day-old challah, about 1 lb, cut into 1 inch thick slices
  - 8 eggs
  - 2 cups milk
  - 1/2 cup heavy cream
  - 1 cup granulated sugar
  - 1/3 cup bourbon
  - 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  - 1 teaspoon freshly ground nutmeg
  - Confectioners' sugar for garnish
  - Maple syrup for serving (optional)
instructions:
  - Generously butter a 2 3/4-quart Dutch oven or deep casserole dish. Arrange bread slices in the dish, overlapping slightly.
  - Whisk together eggs, milk, cream, granulated sugar, bourbon, vanilla, and nutmeg until sugar is dissolved. Pour evenly over bread, turning slices so all absorb liquid. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight.
  - Preheat oven to 350°F.
  - Bake uncovered until puffy and top is lightly browned, about 50 minutes, tenting with foil if the top browns too quickly. Let stand 10 minutes.
  - Dust generously with confectioners' sugar and serve warm with maple syrup alongside.

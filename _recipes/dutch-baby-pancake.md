---
title: Dutch Baby Pancake
dish_type: Breakfast
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Flour
tags:
  - Breakfast
  - American
  - Pancake
description: A puffy oven-baked Dutch baby pancake made in a cast iron skillet. Serve immediately while puffed.
cook_time: 15 min
prep_time: 5 min
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 1 cup milk
  - 1 cup flour
  - 1/2 cup sugar
  - 4 large eggs
  - 8 tablespoons butter (adjust to taste, 4-6 tbsp also works)
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 425°F.
  - Whisk together milk, flour, sugar, and eggs until smooth. Really froth this mixture up — the more frothy, the puffier the pancake.
  - In a large skillet (12x9, cast iron preferred), melt butter over medium heat until bubbly. Tilt pan to coat bottom and sides.
  - Pour pancake batter into the skillet and cook about 1 minute without stirring.
  - Place skillet into oven and bake for about 15 minutes.
  - The pancake is done when golden brown and puffed up. Serve immediately (it will shrink quickly).
  - Serve with syrup or cinnamon sugar.

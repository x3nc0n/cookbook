---
title: Honey Grilled Pork Loin
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Pork
tags:
  - Entrée
  - American
  - Pork
  - Grilled
  - BBQ
description: Butterflied pork loin marinated in soy sauce and ginger, then grilled with a honey and brown sugar glaze.
cook_time: 20-25 min
prep_time: 3h+ (marinating)
servings: 12
ingredients:
  - 1 (3 lb) boneless pork loin roast
  - 2/3 cup soy sauce
  - 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  - 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  - 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  - 1/3 cup honey
  - 1-1/2 tablespoons sesame oil
  - Vegetable oil cooking spray
instructions:
  - Trim fat, butterfly roast by cutting lengthwise to 1/2 inch of other side.
  - Place in dish or large zip-loc bag.
  - Combine soy sauce, ginger, and garlic; pour over roast.
  - Refrigerate 3 hours or more, turning occasionally.
  - Remove roast and discard marinade.
  - Combine brown sugar, honey, and sesame oil in a saucepan. Cook over low heat until sugar dissolves.
  - Coat grill rack with cooking spray. Place roast over medium hot coals (350-400°F).
  - Brush with honey mixture. Cook 20-25 minutes or until meat thermometer reaches 160°F, basting frequently.

---
title: Blackened Salmon
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: Cajun
main_ingredient: Salmon
tags:
  - Entrée
  - Cajun
  - Seafood
  - Salmon
  - Gluten-Free
  - Dairy-Free
description: Spicy blackened salmon fillets with a bold Cajun seasoning crust.
cook_time: 10m
servings: '4'
ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons ground paprika
  - 1 tablespoon ground cayenne pepper
  - 1 tablespoon onion powder
  - 2 teaspoons salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground white pepper
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  - 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
  - 1/4 teaspoon dried basil
  - 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
  - 4 salmon fillets, skin and bones removed
  - Butter for cooking
instructions:
  - In a small bowl, mix paprika, cayenne pepper, onion powder, salt, white pepper, black pepper, thyme, basil and oregano.
  - Brush salmon fillets on both sides with 1/4 cup butter, and sprinkle evenly with the cayenne pepper mixture. Drizzle one side of each fillet with half the remaining butter.
  - In a large, heavy skillet over high heat, cook salmon, butter side down, until blackened, 2 to 5 minutes. Turn fillets, drizzle with remaining butter, and continue cooking until blackened and fish is easily flaked with a fork.

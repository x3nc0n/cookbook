---
title: Chocolate Olive Oil Mug Cake
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chocolate
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Chocolate
  - Quick
  - Microwave
description: A single-serving chocolate mug cake with sumac and white chocolate chunks, ready in minutes.
cook_time: 60-90 sec
servings: 1
ingredients:
  - 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  - 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  - 3 tablespoons sugar
  - 1/2 teaspoon sumac
  - 1/4 teaspoon salt
  - 1 egg
  - 6 tablespoons olive oil
  - 3 tablespoons white chocolate chunks
  - 1 scoop vanilla ice cream (for serving)
instructions:
  - In a mug, mix together the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, sumac, and salt.
  - Add the egg and olive oil and mix until completely blended.
  - Stir in the white chocolate chunks.
  - Microwave for 60-90 seconds, or until desired doneness.
  - Serve with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.

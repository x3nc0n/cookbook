tags:

  - Peanut Butter
description: No-bake protein energy bites with oats, peanut butter, flaxseed, and walnuts.
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 30 min (resting)
servings: 24 balls
ingredients:
  - 1 cup oatmeal (old fashioned organic oats)
  - 1/2 cup gluten-free granola mix
  - 1/2 cup ground flaxseed
  - 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
  - 1/2 cup walnuts (or almonds)
  - 1/4 cup raisins or dried cranberries
  - 1/2 to 3/4 cup maple syrup (or 1/3 cup honey)
  - 1 tablespoon natural cocoa
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla
instructions:
  - Combine all ingredients in a mixing bowl and mix until well blended.
  - Place in the fridge for 30-60 minutes to set up.
  - Remove from fridge and roll into golf ball-sized balls.
  - Enjoy! Store in refrigerator.

---
title: Double-Chocolate Cookies
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chocolate
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Cookies
  - Chocolate
description: Rich double-chocolate cookies with a gooey, creamy center. Don't overbake!
cook_time: 8 min
prep_time: 35 min (includes chilling)
servings: 26
ingredients:
  - 6.75 oz (about 1-1/2 cups) unbleached all-purpose flour
  - 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa
  - 3/8 teaspoon salt
  - 3/4 cup sugar
  - 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
  - 2 tablespoons canola oil
  - 2 large eggs
  - 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 1/2 cup bittersweet chocolate chips
instructions:
  - Combine flour, cocoa, and salt in a bowl, stirring with a whisk.
  - Beat sugar, butter, and oil with a mixer at medium speed until well combined, about 5 minutes. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each. Add vanilla; beat 1 minute. Add flour mixture at low speed just until combined. Add chocolate chips at low speed just until combined. Cover with plastic wrap; chill 30 minutes.
  - Preheat oven to 350°F.
  - Drop dough by 1-1/2 tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart onto parchment-lined baking sheets. Bake for 8 minutes or until almost set.
  - Cool on pan 2 minutes until firm. Remove cookies and cool on wire racks.

---
title: Blackstone Smash Burgers
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Beef
tags:
  - Entrée
  - American
  - Beef
  - Burgers
description: Thin, crispy-edged smash burgers cooked on a hot griddle with melty cheese and classic toppings.
prep_time: 15m
cook_time: 10m
total_time: 25m
servings: "4"
ingredients:
  - 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
  - Salt and black pepper (or burger seasoning), to taste
  - 8 slices American cheese
  - 4 burger buns
  - 1 tbsp butter (optional, for toasting buns)
  - Shredded iceberg lettuce (optional)
  - Sliced tomato (optional)
  - Thin-sliced onion (optional)
  - Pickles (optional)
  - Burger sauce or mayonnaise (optional)
instructions:
  - Preheat a Blackstone griddle (or cast-iron skillet) to medium-high heat, about 425-450°F.
  - Divide the beef into 8 portions (about 2.5-3 ounces each) and gently roll into loose balls.
  - If desired, butter the cut sides of the buns and toast on the griddle until golden; set aside.
  - Place beef balls on the hot griddle with space between each one.
  - Immediately smash each ball into a thin patty using a burger press or sturdy spatula.
  - Season the patties with salt and pepper.
  - Cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until a deep brown crust forms.
  - Scrape under each patty and flip, then top each with a slice of cheese.
  - Cook about 1 more minute until cheese melts and burgers are cooked through.
  - Stack two patties per burger on toasted buns, add desired toppings and sauce, then serve.

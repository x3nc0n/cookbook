---
title: Grilled Chocolate Sandwich
dish_type: Dessert
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chocolate
tags:
  - Dessert
  - American
  - Chocolate
  - Vegetarian
  - Quick
description: A hot, crunchy, melty grilled chocolate sandwich with cinnamon sugar and optional ice cream.
prep_time: 5m
cook_time: 10m
servings: '2'
ingredients:
  - Unsalted butter
  - 4 slices sweet or sourdough French bread
  - About 2 ounces broken or chopped semisweet or bittersweet chocolate (or 1/3 cup chocolate chips)
  - Coarse sea salt
  - 1 tablespoon sugar mixed with 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - Vanilla ice cream (optional)
instructions:
  - Butter one side of all of the slices of bread.
  - In a large skillet, cook the slices butter side-down until golden on one side.
  - Cover two of the slices with chocolate. Place the other two slices on top of the chocolate slices, butter side-up.
  - Cook, flipping the sandwiches as necessary, until the chocolate gets all melty and the sandwiches are browned on both sides.
  - Serve right away with a sprinkling of salt and cinnamon-sugar. Top with a scoop of ice cream if desired.

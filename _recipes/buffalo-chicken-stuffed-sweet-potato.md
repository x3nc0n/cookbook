title: Buffalo Chicken Stuffed Sweet Potato
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Chicken
tags:
  - Entrée
  - American
  - Chicken
  - Slow Cooker
  - Whole30
  - Gluten-Free
  - Dairy-Free
description: Slow cooker buffalo chicken shredded and stuffed inside baked sweet potatoes. Whole30-friendly.
cook_time: 4-6h
prep_time: 10 min
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  - 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  - 1 cup hot pepper sauce (Frank's Red Hot or Sriracha)
  - 1/3 cup ghee (or sub coconut oil or butter)
  - 3 tablespoons coconut aminos
  - 1-1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon cayenne (optional)
  - 8 small baked sweet potatoes (about 7 oz each)
  - Homemade ranch dressing (optional)
instructions:
  - Place chicken in a slow cooker set on low.
  - In a small saucepan over medium-high heat, combine hot sauce, ghee, coconut aminos, garlic powder, and optional cayenne. Stir until ghee is melted.
  - Pour sauce into slow cooker.
  - Cook for 4-6 hours on low or until chicken is tender.
  - Remove chicken, shred with two forks, and return to slow cooker. Toss with sauce.
  - Keep on warm until ready to serve.
  - Serve chicken in baked sweet potatoes, drizzled with buffalo sauce and ranch if desired.

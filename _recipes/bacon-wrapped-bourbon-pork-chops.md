---
title: Bacon Wrapped Bourbon Pork Chops
dish_type: Entrée
cuisine: American
main_ingredient: Pork
tags:
  - Entrée
  - American
  - Pork
  - BBQ
  - Bourbon
description: Thick-cut pork chops marinated in bourbon and brown sugar, wrapped in bacon, and grilled on a Pit Barrel Cooker. Served with Cajun hasselback sweet potatoes.
cook_time: 2h
prep_time: 4h (marinating)
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 2/3 cup bourbon
  - 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  - 6 tablespoons brown sugar
  - 4 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  - 6 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 tablespoons molasses
  - 2 tablespoons ginger, grated
  - 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  - 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon water
  - 3 thick cut pork chops
  - 6 slices bacon
  - "Sweet Potatoes: 2 extra large (or 4 large) sweet potatoes"
  - 3 tablespoons melted butter
  - 2 tablespoons Demerara or brown sugar
  - 1/2 to 3/4 tablespoon Cajun seasoning
instructions:
  - Mix all sauce ingredients (bourbon, soy sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, garlic, molasses, ginger, mustard) in a saucepan. Heat until brown sugar dissolves, then let cool. Divide in half.
  - Marinate pork chops in one half for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  - Light grill/Pit Barrel Cooker. Remove pork chops from marinade and wrap in bacon, securing with toothpicks.
  - Grill pork chops until internal temperature reaches at least 145°F, basting with reserved sauce.
  - Meanwhile, whisk cornstarch slurry into the second half of sauce. Simmer until thickened.
  - Plate pork chops and spoon bourbon sauce on top.
  - "Sweet Potatoes: Cut hasselback-style, place in cast iron pan. Pour melted butter over, working into cuts. Mix sugar and Cajun seasoning; sprinkle over potatoes. Smoke until tender (internal temp ~210°F)."
